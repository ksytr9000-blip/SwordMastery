Sword Mastery PATCH41 cumulative changes
- Statue of Uncertainty (Sewers): Sword Mastery becomes an additional profession-reset choice.
- Existing vanilla profession reset remains available and unchanged.
- Sword Mastery respec costs 1,000,000g by default, can be changed in config.json; executed on the night of sleep. All skill points are refunded, level/EXP and quest unlocks remain.
- All basic skills, active Ohgi, Ultimate Footwork and passive Swordsmanship Apex work only while a real sword is equipped. Daggers/clubs/scythes/tools/empty hands do not qualify.
- Ultimate Footwork costs 1.00% of current maximum Energy by default per use, with no cooldown; configurable in GMCM (0.00%–20.00%, steps of 0.25%).
- All four localizations (EN/KR/JA/ZH) updated. Existing PATCH40 improvements preserved.
